# L'ultima volta — sceneggiatura per inquadrature

Videoclip live action, 90 secondi, 16:9, 24 fps. Stile e protagonisti presi dal clip di riferimento
`riferimenti/shot01-innamorati.mp4`, che diventa la prima inquadratura del video.

## Come usare questi prompt

- I prompt sono in inglese perché i modelli video li seguono meglio.
- A ogni generazione allega **`riferimenti/coppia-riferimento.jpg`** come immagine di riferimento
  (in Higgsfield: Seedance 2.5, modalità `omni_reference`, ruolo `image_references`). Serve a tenere
  uguali i volti e i vestiti in tutte le inquadrature.
- A ogni prompt aggiungi in coda il **blocco STILE** e, se nell'inquadratura compaiono i protagonisti, il **blocco PERSONAGGI**.
- Genera ogni clip 1–2 secondi più lungo del necessario: i tagli si fanno al montaggio, sul beat.
- Imposta la generazione dell'audio su **off**: la colonna sonora è la canzone.

### Blocco STILE (sempre)

```
Cinematic music video shot, 35mm film look, natural golden-hour or practical light, shallow depth of field,
soft handheld camera with subtle shake, warm Mediterranean color grade, gentle film grain, realistic skin,
natural unposed acting, no text, no logos.
```

### Blocco PERSONAGGI (quando compaiono)

```
Same couple as the reference image: a man in his mid-40s with short salt-and-pepper wavy hair, trimmed grey
beard, mustard-yellow V-neck sweater with navy and white trim; a woman around 40 with long dark wavy hair,
white linen shirt, small earrings.
```

Per le scene di sera e di litigio cambia i vestiti in modo coerente: lui camicia blu scura, lei maglione grigio.
Scrivilo nel prompt, altrimenti il modello ripete quelli del riferimento.

### 1 · Innamorati — 0:00–0:05
Il tuo clip, senza modifiche. Lei ride con la testa all'indietro e poi la appoggia su di lui.
Testo in sovrimpressione dal secondo 1,5: **L'ULTIMA VOLTA**.

### 2 · Il mare, il tempo — 0:05–0:09
```
Time-lapse of the same empty beach club terrace facing the sea: the sun sets fast into the horizon,
clouds race across the sky, shadows of the closed parasols sweep across the white tables, waves move quickly.
Static camera, wide shot.
```

### 3 · L'orologio — 0:09–0:12
```
Extreme close-up of an old analog wristwatch on the man's wrist resting on a wooden table by the sea,
the hands spin rapidly forward as in a time-lapse, the golden sunlight on the glass changes to blue dusk.
Slow push-in toward the watch face.
```
Montaggio: taglia sul centro del quadrante e entra nella 5 con uno zoom.

### 4 · Le stagioni al lido — 0:12–0:15
```
The same beach club terrace through the seasons in one continuous shot: summer light, then autumn wind
blowing sand across the tables, then winter rain on stacked chairs and closed parasols. Slow lateral dolly.
```

### 5 · Zoom sulla cucina: inizia il litigio — 0:15–0:20
```
Night, small apartment kitchen lit by a single pendant lamp. Slow zoom in from the doorway. The couple
stands on opposite sides of the kitchen table. She speaks with frustration, gesturing; he leans on the
counter with arms crossed, looking down. Tense, quiet realism. Man in a dark blue shirt, woman in a grey sweater.
```
Testo: *«Non mi ascolti mai.»*

### 6 · Primo piano lei — 0:20–0:24
```
Close-up of the woman in the kitchen at night, lit by a warm pendant lamp, eyes glossy with held-back tears,
she says something short and hurt, then looks away. Handheld, slight shake.
```

### 7 · Primo piano lui — 0:24–0:28
```
Close-up of the man at night in the kitchen, he rubs his face with one hand, exhales, answers without
looking at her, jaw tense. Handheld, shallow focus, background kitchen blurred.
```
Testo: *«Sei sempre altrove.»*

### 8 · In macchina, sotto la pioggia — 0:28–0:34
```
Inside a parked car at night in heavy rain, shot from the back seat between the two front seats. The couple
sits in silence, both looking forward, city lights blurred through the wet windshield, wipers off,
rain streaks moving on the glass. Very still, uncomfortable silence.
```

### 9 · Due stanze — 0:34–0:40
```
Wide shot through a doorway at night: in the foreground the man sits alone on a sofa lit by the TV glow,
in the background through the open balcony door the woman stands alone outside looking at the city.
They don't look at each other. Static camera, slow natural movement.
```
Testo: *Poi, il silenzio.*

### 10 · Si ritrovano al lido — 0:40–0:46
```
Golden hour at the same beach club terrace from the reference image. The woman arrives, the man is already
waiting by the railing. They look at each other, a hesitant smile, then a long quiet hug. Handheld,
warm backlight from the sun on the sea. Summer clothes as in the reference image.
```
Testo: *Per una sera, facemmo finta di niente.*

### 11 · Il ballo — 0:46–0:52
```
The couple dances slowly and barefoot on the empty wooden terrace of the beach club at dusk, string lights
turning on above them, sea behind. She rests her head on his shoulder. Slow circular handheld move around them.
```

### 12 · Fronte contro fronte — 0:52–0:58
```
Tight two-shot at blue hour: foreheads touching, eyes closed. She laughs softly, then her smile breaks into
quiet tears; he holds her face with one hand. Very shallow depth of field, string lights bokeh behind.
```
Testo: *Come la prima volta.*

### 13 · Sulla riva — 0:58–1:04
```
Back view of the couple walking slowly along the shoreline at dusk, holding hands, footprints in the wet sand,
small waves reaching their feet, last light on the horizon. Camera follows behind at a distance.
```

### 14 · Le mani si lasciano — 1:04–1:12
```
Close-up of their two hands holding each other at dusk on the beach; the fingers slowly slide apart and let go.
Then wide shot: they walk away from each other in opposite directions along the shoreline.
```
Se il modello non riesce a fare entrambe le parti, generale come due clip da 4 s.
Testo: *Nessuno dei due si voltò.*

### 15 · Lui si volta. Lei no — 1:12–1:20
```
Medium shot of the man walking alone on the beach at dusk; he stops and turns back to look over his shoulder.
Far in the background the woman keeps walking away without turning. He stays still for a moment, wind in his hair.
```
Testo, con ironia: *Quasi.*

### 16 · Il lido vuoto — 1:20–1:30
```
The empty beach club terrace after sunset, two white chairs side by side facing the sea, one wine glass
left on the table, a light breeze moves the closed parasols. Static wide shot, very slow push-in, fading light.
```
Titolo al centro: **L'ULTIMA VOLTA**, poi *Alcune storie finiscono. Nessuna si cancella.* Dissolvenza al nero.

## Montaggio

- Tagli sui beat della canzone. Il montaggio lo posso fare io quando avrò le clip e il brano.
- Titoli in Space Grotesk Bold maiuscolo, testi in Inter, in basso a sinistra come nei documentari; solo il titolo finale al centro.
- Grading unico su tutte le clip: caldo al lido, freddo con toni blu nelle scene di litigio (5–9), di nuovo caldo dal 10.
- Grana leggera 16 mm e vignetta morbida su tutto, per uniformare clip generate in momenti diversi.
